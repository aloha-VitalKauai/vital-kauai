## Description (4000 chars max)

Vital Kauaʻi is the private member portal of our community on Kauaʻi —
one calm place for preparation, integration, scheduling, and support.

Membership is granted through our application and conversation process.
The app opens with your member credentials, and everything inside is
arranged around where you are in your journey.

WITH THE APP

• Preparation weeks — guided teachings, videos, and practices that ready
  you for your time on island
• Your schedule — ceremony dates, arrival and departure planning, all in
  one view
• Integration — structured support and resources for the weeks and
  months after you return home
• Contributions — complete payments for your in-person services securely
• Gentle reminders — arrival and integration check-ins that meet you
  where you are
• Quick return — Face ID welcomes you back without retyping your
  password

Vital Kauaʻi is offered with care by the founders and facilitators who
hold this work. The portal carries the same content as our web home, in
a form that lives comfortably on your phone.

New here? We welcome you to begin at our website, where you can learn
about the work and submit a membership application.

## App Review notes (paste into App Review Information → Notes)

> Vital Kauaʻi is the private member portal of a religious organization
> on Kauaʻi. Members participate in in-person ceremony on island; the
> app provides their preparation content, scheduling, integration
> support, and account access.
>
> LOGIN: Accounts are provisioned by our staff after an application and
> screening process — the app intentionally offers no self-service
> sign-up, which is why no registration flow appears. Demo credentials
> are provided in the fields above; the demo member is populated with a
> representative journey (scheduled ceremony, preparation weeks,
> integration content). Face ID is an optional re-entry convenience
> layered on this login.
>
> PAYMENTS: All payments in the app are contributions for in-person,
> physical-world services (ceremony participation, lodging) processed by
> Stripe/Square web checkout per Guideline 3.1.3(e). The app sells no
> digital content, no subscriptions, and no substances.
>
> CONTENT: The app provides preparation and integration support for
> members of a religious organization. It contains no consumption
> instructions, no dosage information, and no facilitation of any
> substance sale.
>
> ACCOUNT DELETION: The app offers no in-app account creation
> (Guideline 5.1.1(v) keys on account creation); members may request
> account removal through the support contact at any time.
